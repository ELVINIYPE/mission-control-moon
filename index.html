Mission Control: MOON

Plan a real trip to the Moon. Choose your rocket, launch it, land on the Moon and bring your crew home safely, using real NASA science.

🛠️ 1 Design→ 🚀 2 Launch→ 🌙 3 Land→ 🏠 4 Come home
👩‍🚀 1 to 4 players · 💰 $5 billion budget · 🎓 real numbers from Apollo
Earth and Moon not to scale · drag your mouse to look around

❓ How to play

👩‍🚀
1. Pick your crew

Play alone or with up to 4 friends. Each player gets a job, like real Mission Control.

🛠️
2. Design the mission

Choose a rocket and spacecraft. Too heavy and it won't fly; too big and you waste money!

🚀
3. Fly it

Launch, aim for where the Moon WILL be, and slow down to land softly.

🏠
4. Come home

Bring everyone home safe. Then see WHY your mission worked or failed.
